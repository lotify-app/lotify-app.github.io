Privacy Policy of Lotifybot

Effective Date: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use, store, and protect it. We respect your privacy and comply with the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679, as well as the legislation of Ukraine.

1. General Provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (hereinafter – "Policy") regulates the procedure for collecting, processing, storing, and protecting personal data of users of the Lotifybot service (bot and website). By using our service, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

1.2. By using our service, you provide voluntary consent to the processing of your personal data in accordance with this Policy.

1.3. We do not collect or process personal data of persons under 16 years of age without the consent of parents or guardians.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary for the operation of the service:

  • Identification: your unique Telegram ID – for user identification in the system, providing access to the bot, and setting up targeting.
  • Settings: filters you set (keywords, price, region, categories) – for monitoring listings and sending you relevant notifications.
  • Technical: data about interaction with the bot (request times, types of actions) – for improving the service, error diagnostics, and analytics.
  • Payment: we DO NOT collect or DO NOT process payment data (card numbers, bank accounts). All payments are made through third-party payment systems (MonoBank, Apple Pay, Google Pay, etc.), which have their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Data

3.1. Your data is used exclusively for:

  • providing access to the service and its functions;
  • sending notifications based on your specified criteria;
  • improving the bot's operation (analytics, technical support);
  • ensuring system security and preventing abuse.

3.2. We DO NOT transfer your data to third parties, except as expressly provided by law or to ensure the operation of the service (for example, hosting providers that provide technical infrastructure). All partners guarantee data confidentiality.

3.3. We store your data for as long as necessary for the operation of the service, or until you request deletion. The maximum storage period is 12 months after the last activity. After that, the data is anonymized or deleted.

4. Where We Store Your Data

  • Your settings and identifier are stored in a secure cloud infrastructure (in the EU or in countries that provide an adequate level of data protection).
  • We do not store message history or correspondence in the bot.
  • All data is stored only for the time necessary to perform the service's functions.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject (GDPR)

In accordance with GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: obtain information about what data we store about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: request the deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction of processing: restrict the processing of your data.
  • Data portability: obtain a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to the processing of your data for marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the contacts provided in Section 9.

6. Data Protection

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or destruction:

  • data encryption during transmission (SSL/TLS);
  • restricted access to data only for authorized personnel;
  • regular monitoring and updating of security systems;
  • use of reliable cloud services with ISO 27001 certification.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website uses a minimal set of cookies exclusively for:

  • ensuring website functionality (saving language selection);
  • analytics (Google Analytics) – if you have given consent.

We use only technical cookies to save language selection. Analytical cookies (Google Analytics) are used only with your consent. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

8. Links to Third-Party Sites

Our service contains links to third-party platforms (OLX, eBay, Willhaben, etc.). We are not responsible for their privacy policies. We recommend that you review them separately.

If you have questions about this Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us:

10. Changes to the Policy

We may update this Policy. We will notify you of material changes through the website or the bot. The current version is always available on the /privacy.html page (or the corresponding language version).

11. Jurisdiction

This Policy is governed by the legislation of Ukraine. Disputes arising in connection with this Policy are resolved in court in Ukraine.
